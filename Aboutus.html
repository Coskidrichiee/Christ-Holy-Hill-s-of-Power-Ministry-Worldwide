Our Mission

A Place of Encounter and Empowerment.

(Exodus 3:1-10)

1. A place of Prophetic Encounter, Holy Ghost Encounter and Holy Encounter. (Acts 2:1-4 $ Matthew 17:1-9).

2. A place of Spiritual, Mental and Moral Development and Empowerment. (Act 2:1-7 $ Exodus 4:1-9).

3. A place of divers Signs and Wonders. (Act 2:1-7 $ Exodus 4:1-9).

4. The Place of His Presence and His Power experience. (Mathew 17:1-18).

5. The Place of Salvation and Love. ( 2 Corinthians 3:17 ).

6. A place of Restoration to Destiny and Glory. ( Exodus 3:1-12 ).

7. A place of Divine Visitation. ( Daniel 10 : 1_21 ).

8. A place of Destiny Discover. ( Genesis 28 : 10-18 ).

9. A place of Divine Encounter of His Love and Mercy. ( Psalm 3 : 4-5 ).

10. The place of His Purifying fire and Power. ( Obadiah 1: 18 ).

11. The place of Encountering Liberation Power.

Our Mandate

Go and Create and atmosphere for me to have Encounter with my people to bring them from the land of capitivity to their land of rest and fulfillment of Destiny.

Our vision

To create an atmosphere, place and Environment of Encounter with God and his Spirit where God's people can discover their true identity in Christ Jesus and be Spiritually, Mentally and Physically Empowered with the fulfillment of Destiny and Purpose, where their God Ordained Purpose can be Unvailed to them through Spiritual Encounters, Vision, Revelation Angelic and Prophetic Encounter in Prayer and in God's Word, Via study and Meditation.
