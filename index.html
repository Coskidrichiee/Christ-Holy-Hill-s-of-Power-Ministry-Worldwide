Meet Our Pastor

PROPHET JOHN AUDU

Senior Pastor

+234 903 843 1309

Prophet John Audu is a dedicated servant of God, leading Christ Holy-Hill's of Power Ministry Worldwide with passion, integrity, and deep spiritual insight. Called into ministry over two decades ago, he has committed his life to preaching the undiluted word of God and empowering believers to discover and fulfill their divine purpose.

Under his leadership, the ministry has grown tremendously, becoming a place of transformation, healing, and spiritual revival. Prophet Audu is known for his prophetic accuracy, compassion for souls, and unrelenting focus on prayer and the Word.

He believes strongly in raising leaders, restoring broken lives, and building a generation of spiritually grounded individuals who will impact their communities for Christ. His teachings are rooted in love, wisdom, and truth, with an emphasis on practical Christian living.
