Our Mission

A Place of Encounter and Empowerment.

(Exodus 3:1-10)

  • A place of Prophetic Encounter, Holy Ghost Encounter and Holy Encounter. (Acts 2:1-4 & Matthew 17:1-9)
  • A place of Spiritual, Mental and Moral Development and Empowerment. (Acts 2:1-7 & Exodus 4:1-9)
  • A place of divers Signs and Wonders. (Acts 2:1-7 & Exodus 4:1-9)
  • The Place of His Presence and His Power experience. (Matthew 17:1-18)
  • The Place of Salvation and Love. (2 Corinthians 3:17)
  • A place of Restoration to Destiny and Glory. (Exodus 3:1-12)
  • A place of Divine Visitation. (Daniel 10:1-21)
  • A place of Destiny Discovery. (Genesis 28:10-18)
  • A place of Divine Encounter of His Love and Mercy. (Psalm 3:4-5)
  • The place of His Purifying fire and Power. (Obadiah 1:18)
  • The place of Encountering Liberation Power.

Our Mandate

Go and create an atmosphere for Me to have an encounter with My people to bring them from the land of captivity to their land of rest and fulfillment of destiny.

Our Vision

To create an atmosphere, place, and environment of encounter with God and His Spirit where God's people can discover their true identity in Christ Jesus and be spiritually, mentally, and physically empowered for the fulfillment of destiny and purpose. Where their God-ordained purpose can be unveiled through spiritual encounters, visions, revelations, angelic and prophetic encounters in prayer and in God's Word through study and meditation.
