Prophetic and Power Service

Wednesday Midweek | 4pm - 7pm

Prophetic and Power Service is a church gathering focused on the supernatural—featuring prophecy, healing, deliverance, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

What Happens During the Service?
  • Prophetic Utterances
  • Healing and Deliverance
  • Demonstration of God’s Power
  • Impartation of Spiritual Gifts
  • Worship and Atmosphere of Glory
Importance in Christian Life:
  • Divine Direction and Clarity
  • Healing and Restoration
  • Spiritual Breakthroughs
  • Faith Strengthening
  • Revival and Fresh Anointing
  • Protection from the Enemy

Monthly Program: Night of Total Deliverance and Power

This is a monthly all-night service with intense worship, prayer, and deliverance encounters.

Key Points:
  • Spiritual Preparation: Fasting, prayer, and intercession
  • Worship: High-energy praise and deep worship
  • Prophetic Declarations: Victory, healing, breakthrough
  • Deliverance and Healing Prayers: Break strongholds and heal the sick
  • Testimonies: Share past miracles and release new prophetic words

Word Encounters and Communion Service

Friday | 5pm - 7pm

Holy Communion is a sacred practice that commemorates Christ’s sacrifice and our unity with Him.

Biblical References:
  • Matthew 26:26-28: The institution of Communion
  • 1 Corinthians 11:24-29: Paul’s reminder to partake reverently
  • John 6:53-56: The promise of eternal life
Spiritual Benefits:
  • Remembrance of Christ’s Sacrifice
  • Spiritual Nourishment
  • Unity with Believers
  • Proclamation of Faith
  • Preparation for Eternal Life

Sunday Services

Every Sunday is a time for worship, teaching, prayer, and communion in fellowship with others.

Key Aspects:
  • Worship and Praise: Psalm 95:1-2
  • Bible Teaching: 2 Timothy 4:2
  • Prayer: Matthew 18:20
  • Fellowship: Hebrews 10:24-25
  • Communion: 1 Corinthians 11:24-25
  • Preaching and Exhortation: Romans 10:14

Why It Matters: Sunday worship builds spiritual strength, community, and obedience to God’s Word.

Counseling and Prophetic Encounters

Every first Sunday | 2pm - 10pm

One-on-one prophetic counseling that brings personal guidance, healing, and deliverance.

Key Importance:
  • Clarity in Life’s Decisions
  • Deliverance from Spiritual Battles
  • Healing and Emotional Restoration
  • Strengthens Relationship with God
  • Encouragement & Spiritual Growth

House Fellowship

After Sunday Service

Small group meetings in homes for Bible study, worship, and mutual support.

Key Features:
  • Intimate Fellowship: Hebrews 10:24-25
  • Bible Study & Prayer: Acts 2:46-47
  • Support & Encouragement: Galatians 6:2
  • Practical Application: James 1:22
  • Community Building: 1 John 4:12
Benefits:
  • Personal Growth
  • Community Connection
  • Practical Support

House fellowships deepen faith, strengthen bonds, and complement Sunday worship.
